Write a brief opening that identifies the problem, explains why it matters, and tells the reader what the article will cover.

## The main problem or question

Explain the situation in plain language. Keep paragraphs relatively short so the article is easy to scan.

Include important context such as:

- Who is affected
- Why the issue matters
- Common misconceptions
- Business or security implications

## What businesses should consider

Explain the most important considerations before making a decision.

### First consideration

Provide specific, useful guidance instead of general statements.

### Second consideration

Explain how this consideration affects business operations, cost, security, reliability, or productivity.

### Third consideration

Mention any warning signs or common mistakes the reader should avoid.

## Recommended approach

Provide a practical sequence the reader can follow:

1. Assess the current situation.
2. Identify the business requirements.
3. Evaluate the available options.
4. Document the decision.
5. Review the results after implementation.

## When professional assistance may help

Explain when the reader can reasonably handle the issue internally and when outside expertise may reduce risk, downtime, or unnecessary expense.

## Final thoughts

Summarize the main recommendation in two or three paragraphs. Do not introduce an entirely new subject in the conclusion.
